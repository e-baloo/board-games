# Brass: Birmingham rules (English extract)

Source: [official English rulebook](../brass-birmingham/rules/64-brass-birmingham-rulebook-1.pdf)

This is a structured gameplay extract for fast rule lookup. The official rulebook remains authoritative for diagrams, examples, iconography, and edge cases.

## Objective and structure

- The game has two eras: the Canal Era (1770-1830) and the Rail Era (1830-1870).
- Each era is played in rounds until both the Draw Deck and every player's Hand are exhausted.
- There are 8, 9, or 10 rounds per era in a 4-, 3-, or 2-player game.
- The winner is the player with the most Victory Points (VP) after Rail Era scoring.
- At the end of each era, players score their Link tiles and flipped Industry tiles.

## Components

- Board, 4 Player Mats, 4 Character tiles, 4 VP markers, 4 Income markers, and player Link/Industry tiles.
- 8 Wild cards: 4 Wild Location and 4 Wild Industry.
- 64 Location and Industry cards.
- Coal cubes, Iron cubes, Beer barrels, Money tokens, and 9 Merchant tiles.
- Industry tiles: Cotton Mills, Coal Mines, Iron Works, Manufacturers, Potteries, and Breweries.

## Setup

### Board

1. Choose either side of the board.
2. With fewer than four players, remove cards and Merchant tiles marked for a higher player count.
3. Place the Wild Location and Wild Industry cards face up in their card areas.
4. Shuffle the remaining cards and place them face down as the Draw Deck.
5. Shuffle Merchant tiles and place one face up on each Merchant space allowed by the player count. At two players, do not place Merchants in Warrington or Nottingham. At three players, do not place one in Nottingham.
6. Place one Beer barrel beside each Beer space beside a non-blank Merchant.
7. Fill the Coal Market except for one £1 space.
8. Fill the Iron Market except for both £1 spaces.
9. Put remaining cubes and Beer in the General Supply.
10. Put Money beside the board as the Bank.

Location cards in the Draw Deck depend on player count: at two players, blue and teal Location cards are excluded; at three players, teal Location cards are excluded; at four players, all Location cards are included.

### Player areas

1. Take a Player Mat and £17.
2. Choose a Character tile.
3. Take the matching Link tiles.
4. Stack your matching Industry tiles on the appropriate Player Mat spaces, face down with the black/VP side hidden.
5. Put your VP marker on 0 and Income marker on 10.
6. Draw 8 cards as your Hand. Draw one additional card face down as your initial Discard Pile.
7. Shuffle all Character tiles and place them randomly on the Turn Order Track.

## Round and turn sequence

- Players act in the order of their Character tiles on the Turn Order Track.
- On a turn, perform 2 actions. In the first Canal Era round, perform only 1 action.
- Each action requires discarding one card from your Hand. Passing also requires a discard.
- A normal discarded card goes face up on your Discard Pile. A Wild card returns face up to its Card Draw Area.
- You may perform the same action twice in one turn.
- After your actions, refill your Hand to 8 cards while cards remain in the Draw Deck.
- Put all Money spent during your turn on your Character tile. This Money determines next round's turn order.
- When the Draw Deck is empty, players start each round with fewer cards until Hands are empty.

### End of round

1. Determine next round's order by Money spent this round: least spent goes first, most spent goes last. Ties preserve the previous relative order.
2. Return all Money from Character tiles to the Bank.
3. Collect Income from the Bank equal to your current Income level, except after the final round of the game.
4. If Income is negative, pay that amount to the Bank. If unable to pay, remove your Industry tiles from the board for half their printed cost, rounded down, until the shortfall is covered. You may keep excess Money. If the shortfall remains, lose 1 VP per unpaid £1, if possible.

## Actions

### Build

Build an Industry tile on the board.

1. Discard the required card face up: a Location card for the named location, or an Industry card matching the tile type. A Wild Location or Wild Industry card may substitute. A Location card can build any Industry at that location, even outside your network; an Industry card requires the location to be in your network.
2. Take the lowest-level tile of the chosen industry from your Player Mat. Place it face down on an eligible empty space, black/VP side hidden.
3. Pay the printed cost by placing Money on your Character tile. Consume required Coal and Iron.
4. If you built a Coal Mine or Iron Works, immediately move its printed number of resource cubes to the matching Market, filling the most expensive spaces first. Gain the printed amount for each cube moved. If the last cube leaves the tile, flip it and advance Income by the printed amount.
5. If you built a Brewery, place 1 Beer in the Canal Era or 2 Beer in the Rail Era.

Building restrictions:

- In the Canal Era, you may have at most one of your Industry tiles per location, although several players may occupy the same location.
- In the Rail Era, multiple Industry tiles may occupy one location, but never the same space.
- A tile with the Rail Era restriction icon to the left of its Player Mat slot cannot be built during the Rail Era.
- Level 1 Potteries are an exception and may be built during the Rail Era.
- You may build in an opponent-occupied location if the relevant space is available.
- During the Canal Era, you cannot build your restricted tiles until they are removed by Develop.

### Overbuilding

- You may replace one of your own Industry tiles with a higher-level tile of the same industry at any time, paying the new tile's cost. Return resources on the old tile to the General Supply.
- You may replace an opponent's Coal Mine or Iron Works only with a higher-level tile of the same type, and only when no resource of that type remains on any matching Industry tile or in its Market.
- Remove the replaced tile from the game. Its owner does not lose Income or VP; the removed tile does not score later.

### Network

Place Link tiles to expand your network.

- Canal Era: build one Canal Link for £3. Rail Links are forbidden.
- Rail Era: build one Rail Link for £5, or two Rail Links for £15 and consume 1 Beer from a Brewery. Merchant Beer cannot pay for this bonus.
- Discard any card; Wild cards return to their Card Draw Area.
- Place each Link on an undeveloped printed line. It must be adjacent to a location already in your network, unless you have no Industry or Link tiles on the board; then the first Link may go on any undeveloped line.
- Each Rail Link requires 1 Coal. A Rail Link must be connected to a Coal source after it is placed. If using another player's Brewery for the two-Link Beer cost, that Brewery must be connected to the second Link after placement.
- A location is in your network if it contains one of your Industry tiles or is adjacent to one of your Link tiles.
- Two locations are connected if any continuous route of Links joins them, regardless of ownership.

### Develop

Remove 1 or 2 Industry tiles from your Player Mat to access higher-level tiles.

- Discard any card; Wild cards return to their Card Draw Area.
- For each tile removed, pay 1 Iron. Remove the lowest-level remaining tile of the chosen industry type; the two tiles do not need to be the same type.
- Tiles are returned to the box and do not score.
- Potteries with the lightbulb icon cannot be removed by Develop. They must be built first; use Build to clear them from the Player Mat.

### Sell

Flip built Cotton Mills, Manufacturers, and Potteries.

1. Discard any card; Wild cards return to their Card Draw Area.
2. Choose an unflipped Cotton Mill, Manufacturer, or Pottery connected to a Merchant showing that industry's icon.
3. Provide the Beer shown in the tile's upper-right corner. Beer may come from your unflipped Breweries without a connection, an opponent's connected unflipped Brewery, or the Beer space beside the Merchant being used.
4. Flip the Industry tile and advance Income by the number of spaces shown in its lower-right corner.
5. Resolve the Merchant Beer bonus, if the adjacent Beer barrel was used.
6. Repeat steps 2-5 for additional eligible tiles if you can pay all requirements.

You cannot Sell unless all required Beer can be supplied.

Merchant Beer bonuses:

- Gloucester: Develop by removing the lowest-level tile of any industry from your Player Mat.
- Oxford: Advance your Income marker by 2 spaces.
- Nottingham and Shrewsbury: Gain the VP amount shown by the Merchant.
- Warrington: Take £5 from the Bank.

### Loan

- Discard any card; Wild cards return to their Card Draw Area.
- Take £30 from the Bank.
- Move your Income marker backward by 3 Income levels, not 3 spaces.
- The resulting Income level cannot be below -10.

### Scout

- Discard 3 cards from your Hand.
- Take 1 Wild Location and 1 Wild Industry card.
- You cannot Scout if you already have a Wild card in your Hand.
- Wild cards can be used as any Location or Industry card and return to their Card Draw Areas when discarded.

## Resources and markets

### Coal

Coal is required for Rail Links and some Industry tiles.

1. If connected, use the closest unflipped Coal Mine owned by any player. If it runs out, use the next closest one. This is free.
2. Otherwise, buy from the Coal Market, starting with the cheapest cube. You must be connected to a Coal Market icon. If the Market is empty, Coal costs £8 per cube.
3. Return consumed Coal to the General Supply.

### Iron

Iron is required for Develop and some Industry tiles. The consuming tile does not need to be connected to Iron.

1. Use Iron from any unflipped Iron Works owned by any player. Each cube may come from a different Iron Works. This is free.
2. Otherwise, buy from the Iron Market, starting with the cheapest cube. If the Market is empty, Iron costs £6 per cube.
3. Return consumed Iron to the General Supply.

### Beer

- Your own unflipped Breweries do not need to be connected.
- An opponent's unflipped Brewery must be connected to the location where the Beer is needed.
- Merchant Beer may only be used during Sell.
- Return consumed Beer to the General Supply.

## Industry flipping and Income

- Flipped tiles have a black top half and a VP icon. Only flipped Industry tiles score their printed VP at era scoring.
- Cotton Mills, Manufacturers, and Potteries flip through Sell.
- Coal Mines, Iron Works, and Breweries flip when their last resource or Beer is removed, even during another player's turn.
- When Income increases, advance the Income marker by the number of spaces shown, not by the number of Income levels. Income cannot exceed level 30.

## Era end scoring

An era ends after the round in which all players use the last cards in their Hands.

1. Score Link tiles: for each of your Links, gain 1 VP for each VP icon shown in adjacent locations. Remove scored Links from the board.
2. Score all your flipped Industry tiles by their printed VP. Unflipped tiles score nothing.
3. At the end of the Canal Era only, remove all level 1 Industry tiles from the board and return them to the box. Level 2 and higher tiles remain. Do not remove tiles from Player Mats.
4. At the end of the Canal Era only, place 1 Beer barrel on every empty Beer space beside a non-blank Merchant.
5. Shuffle all player Discard Piles together and place them as the new Draw Deck. Turn over the bottom card of each pile first, because it was placed face down.

After Rail Era scoring, the player with the most VP wins. Ties are broken by highest Income, then most Money; if still tied, tied players draw.

## Connected locations and special cases

- A Location card or Wild Location card lets you build at that named location without network access.
- If you have no Industry or Link tiles on the board, your first Link may be placed anywhere on an undeveloped line.
- Two unnamed locations are Farm Breweries. They have one Brewery space each and accept only Brewery cards or Wild Industry cards.
- Cannock connects to the Farm Brewery on its left through a Link. A Link between Kidderminster and Worcester also connects both locations to their Farm Brewery; no second Link is allowed there.
- You may never sell Coal or Iron to a Market after the Build action that created the Industry tile.

## Quick reminders

- Discard one card for every action, including Pass.
- The same action may be taken twice in one turn.
- Income level is not Victory Points in the normal game.
- Loans are never repaid, but they reduce Income.
- A location normally must be in your network to build there unless the played card specifically names that location.
